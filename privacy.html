Privacy

A clear overview of data used to operate Vision SMP.

Information used by the network

Vision SMP may process Minecraft usernames, UUIDs, gameplay records, moderation records, server logs, support messages and purchase fulfilment information needed to operate the service.

Why information is used

Information is used to provide gameplay features, maintain security, prevent abuse, deliver purchases, answer support requests and improve network reliability.

Payments

Payment checkout is processed through Tebex. Vision SMP will never ask players to send card details through Minecraft chat or Discord messages.

Sharing

Information may be handled by service providers required to operate hosting, payments, moderation or support. Vision SMP does not need to publish private account information to other players.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the support methods listed on the Support page.
